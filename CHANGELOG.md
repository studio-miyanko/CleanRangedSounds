# CleanRangedSounds

## Unreleased

- Renamed from Clean Bow Gun Sounds. Settings start fresh under the new name.
- Every sound file has its own Mute row with a Play button, so you can hear a sound before you mute it.
- Bows, crossbows and guns are covered in every phase with a sound: bow Pull and Shot, crossbow Load and Shot, gun Load and Shot, including the hunter spell variants Forever's newer shots play.
- Replace Muted Shot Sounds keeps your own shots audible when you mute only some shot sounds of your weapon. It replaces Cleaner Bow Release: mute bow Shot 2 for the cleaner release.
- Unticking a sound always brings it back, also after a reload.
- Opening the settings in combat opens them once combat ends.
